COMPAQPORTABLE III
Cable speed

Type KERMIT at the A> prompt to connect to the Pi, just as on the real Compaq. Ctrl+] then C goes to the Kermit prompt, where CONNECT goes back and EXIT returns to DOS. Once connected, this is the Pi's shared screen: the same one the real Compaq and http://compaq.local show.
